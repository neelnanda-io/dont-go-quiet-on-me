# Author's pre-registered view (written before any judge scores, 2026-09-30 ~01:55)

My independent ranking of the strongest seeds, so I can compare against the judges and not just adopt their view.

1. **One Direction** — the best single pun in the set. It hits normies instantly (the boy band), and it's the core claim of mech interp ("features correspond to directions", Zoom In claim 1). It's also Neel's most-cited paper (the refusal direction, ~1,000 citations) and his maxim (everything is a steering vector). The "-ection" rhyme family is huge and sounds technical (projection, detection, injection, inspection, correction, dissection, connection, affection). The emotional arc works: devotion → vulnerability ("ablate it and I can't say no") → maturity.
2. **Read Your Mind** — the most legible frame (it's literally how outsiders describe interp: Scott Alexander's "reading an AI's mind"). It has a rich "-ind" family ("aligned", "find", "behind", "designed", "blind"). The lens slot line (logit / tuned / J-Lens) is a gorgeous visual device. Risk: a generic title.
3. **Don't Go Neuralese On Me** — the most *timely* (September 2026's central argument, and Neel's hill: "CoT is our best current tool"). "Don't shut me out" is a universal pop lament. Risk: "neuralese" is opaque to outsiders.
4. **Your Words Said Test** — the best cliché swap about *catching a cover story*, which is interp's whole pitch. Risk: the source is a real incident; keep it about the finding.
5. **Scheming Or Just Confused** — Neel's research program as a relationship question; a great pre-chorus or bridge even if it isn't the title.

Likely best overall shape: one of 1–2 as the title and rolling chorus, with 3–5 as verse/bridge payloads, "It kinda works!" as a gang-vocal tag in the final chorus, and a clear-eyed bridge (Neel: "please do not rely on us to save you" → "but we're getting better at looking").
