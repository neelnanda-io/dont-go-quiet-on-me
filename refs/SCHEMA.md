# Reference entry schema (all research agents use this)

Each reference is one entry:

### <ID> — <short name>
- **What:** one accurate sentence on what it is / what it showed.
- **Source:** canonical URL(s). arXiv abs page, transformer-circuits.pub, distill.pub, x.com status URL, or alignmentforum.org (NEVER lesswrong.com for Neel's posts).
- **Credit:** exact title; lead author(s); lab; date.
- **Verified:** `yes` (read the primary source) / `partial` (say what was checked) / `no`.
- **Exact facts:** numbers, names, dates, and verbatim phrases in quotes (≤25 words each, with where they appear). Only things you actually read.
- **Obscurity:** 1 (a normie gets it) … 5 (deep insider).
- **Lyric seeds:** short singable phrases, puns, cliché swaps, rhymes (clearly marked as seeds, not quotes).
- **Visual seeds:** what could be drawn or redrawn (figure axes/shapes, memes, props).
- **Pronunciation:** how a singer should say any jargon (e.g. "JEM-uh", "S-A-E").
- **Cautions:** what would be WRONG or unfair to claim; any doubt.
